About the model

The skeleton is a 10-segment sagittal model with shoulder and hip abduction; muscles are curves between textbook attachment sites scaled to a 175 cm male. Load combines gravity torque, hand load and posture tone; length is the attachment distance against the neutral pose, damped for muscles that wrap a ball joint; pain risk grows with load held over time, with shortening and with surface pressure. Twisting and side-bending are not modelled.

Anatomy: Gray's Anatomy (41st ed.) and Moore's Clinically Oriented Anatomy. Exercise doses: ACSM Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription (10th ed.). Tight/weak grouping: Janda's crossed syndromes (a clinical framework with mixed evidence). The content has not been reviewed by a clinician.
